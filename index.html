Approach

Simple systems

I favour simple, maintainable architectures and the smallest amount of complexity a product needs. That usually means server-rendered HTML, progressive enhancement and a small amount of JavaScript.

Pragmatic choices

I am technology-agnostic professionally. I have worked in JavaScript, TypeScript, PHP and C#, and reach for Go on independent products when a single binary and a small dependency surface make sense.

AI-assisted, human-reviewed

I use AI coding agents to move faster, while keeping changes small and reviewable. Tests, version control and code review remain the checks that decide whether work is finished.
