Building the Share a Coke Customisation Experience

Front-end developer at M10c · Oct 2024 - Jun 2025

At M10c, I built React and TypeScript components for three applications supporting Coca-Cola’s international Share a Coke campaign. My main contribution was the customer-facing flow for personalising cans.

Handling different customer journeys

The customisation steps varied by market and by how customers bought their personalised cans. The flow needed to show the relevant steps, apply local name validation rules, and preserve or reset selections as customers moved through it.

I implemented this behaviour using React hooks and lookup objects.

Building and reviewing the interface

I built personalisation forms, a 2D product preview, and responsive, multilingual layouts. The lead developer reviewed and refined the state logic, and dedicated testers manually checked the flows before launch.

Delivery

The customisation flow was delivered as part of the international Share a Coke campaign. The campaign has since ended; the screenshot below shows the original interface.
