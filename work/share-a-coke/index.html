Building the Share a Coke Customisation Experience

Front-end developer at M10c · Oct 2024 - Jun 2025

React and TypeScript interfaces supporting different markets, personalisation flows and purchase environments.

  • React/TypeScript Contributed to a consumer customisation app and two internal market-configuration tools.
  • Configuration Market data drove steps, name validation and which selections persisted.
  • Commerce Basket and Stripe checkout for online orders, alongside in-store journeys.

Overview

Share a Coke let customers personalise a Coke can with a name or message in participating markets. I was one of the front-end developers at M10c on the React and TypeScript applications behind the campaign. The consumer customisation experience was the largest part of my work; the other two applications were internal tools for configuring markets.

The engineering challenge

The customisation experience was not a single linear form. Each market could define its own steps, name validation and allow-lists, and whether the customer was in an in-store or out-of-store experience. Online orders added a basket and Stripe checkout. Selections also behaved differently between steps: some values persisted, others reset as users moved between steps. Hard-coding a path per market would have meant a separate flow for every variation.

My implementation

I built the personalisation forms, 2D product previews, multilingual interfaces and responsive layouts. I used React hooks and lookup objects, with a state-machine-like structure rather than a library: market configuration decided which steps and branches applied, and each step defined whether earlier data persisted or reset. The lead developer reviewed the approach and modified the final state-management implementation. I also built a Three.js 3D can prototype, which was not shipped.

Delivery and collaboration

Work came from supplied designs and scoped requirements. I collaborated with the lead developer and designer, who reviewed and refined my implementations. Two dedicated testers carried out manual testing.

Outcome and limits

The campaign launched internationally, with market-specific experiences configured through the internal tools. I did not measure conversion, performance or reach, so this page claims no commercial results. The original customer page is no longer live; the screenshot below is historical evidence.
