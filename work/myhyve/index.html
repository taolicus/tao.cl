Case study

MyHyve Ticketing Portal

Mid-Level Engineer at Hyve Managed Hosting · Sep 2025 - Oct 2026

A monthly support-ticket automation for stray orders at Hyve Managed Hosting, built into the production Angular and C# ticketing portal.

The problem

Stray orders needed a monthly check to become support tickets. Some maintenance tasks were recorded under the wrong category, so the team did not see them as maintenance work.

What I built

I built and deployed the workflow across the Angular portal and its C# backend. On each monthly run it checks for stray orders, creates a ticket for each one and sends the ticket to support review. Customer Experience validated the workflow after rollout.

After rollout

The workflow surfaced maintenance work the team had been missing. It also gives each stray order a repeatable route into a support ticket and a support-review step.

Separate example: modernising the portal

The monthly workflow sat alongside a wider modernisation of the same portal. I led the Angular 19→21 upgrade and cut npm audit findings from 50+ to under 5, including three of four critical issues. The portal adopted Angular signals and rxResource, and I replaced legacy Sass with Tailwind. I also reorganised related components into feature-oriented modules.

This case study uses only details that can be stated without internal customer, order or ticket data.
